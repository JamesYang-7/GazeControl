# 3People-2022 → SMPL-X and study 2 — build record

Converting the three-party corpus, picking and exporting its segments, and how study 2 is built on study 1's code. `3people-motion-sources.md` has the source roots and formats; `study2-design.md` is the study design.

> Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-09-11 so that file could stay a high-level guide. This is the design record — decisions, the reasons behind them and the dates they were taken — so keep it current when the behaviour it describes changes.

## 3People-2022 → SMPL-X (in progress)

A second corpus is being converted to SMPL-X: **3People-2022**, a three-party (not dyadic) conversation capture with speech, motion and gaze. It is unrelated to TalkingWithHands and shares none of its conventions. **`Assets/Docs/3people-motion-sources.md` is the entry point** — the four source roots, the file formats, the conversion plan and the traps. Read it before touching this work; the summary below is only enough to know when it applies.

Four roots, all outside this repository, written with symbols the way `{PAPER_ROOT}` is:

- `{TPC_CODE}` = `F:\Code\Multi-TPC` — the dataset's own processing repo and documentation.
- `{TPC_DATA}` = `D:\3People-2022` — the processed exports (motion txt, gaze, audio, captions, annotations).
- `{TPC_VICON}` = `D:\3People-2024\3Party-Conversation-2022` — the original Vicon project: C3D markers and per-participant VSK skeleton templates. Upstream of everything in `{TPC_DATA}/mocap`.
- `{TPC_VIEWER}` = `F:\Code\Conversation_Demo` — the C++/OpenGL reference viewer; the authority on how the motion export is interpreted.

Three things that will otherwise cost a session:

- **`Separate/..._PC_<N>_...` is mislabelled.** The `N` is an alphabetical position, not a participant number, and it is wrong in 16 of 21 slots. Gaze, audio and `raw_data/` carry true PC numbers; the processed motion does not. Join on the subject name via `mocap/<date>/Notes.xlsx`, which is the participant register **and** the per-stream sync sheet.
- **The two reference readers in `{TPC_VIEWER}` use different Euler orders** (`Data/main.cpp` XYZ, `Demo/main.cpp` XZY). **It is XYZ, measured**: fitting the C3D markers to the posed source skeleton leaves 2-6 mm under XYZ and 40-300 mm under every other order (`--check-markers`). Still behind one named constant. Marker-based fitting from `{TPC_VICON}` remains the higher-fidelity route; **angle retargeting is the chosen first route** (user, 2026-09-07) and is built.
- **The source has 19 joints against SMPL-X's 55** — no neck, no spine subdivision, no fingers. Jaw and eyes must stay zero in the clip: LipSync and `IGazePolicy` drive them at runtime.

**The converter is `Tools/convert_3people_smplx.py`** (2026-09-07): all 69 participant-sessions are at `F:\Data\3People-2022-SMPLX\<date>\Session_<S>_pc<N>_<Name>.npz`, room frame horizontally, grounded vertically, default body. Its docstring is the design record. It reads the stacked CSV (never the `Separate/` txt — the CSV block order is not always alphabetical) and resolves the participant through `Notes.xlsx`. The C3D markers have gaps stored as zero points; the published `gazeBehavior.txt` cannot be reproduced from the files on disk and is not relied on.

**Segments come from `Tools/find_3people_segments.py`** (2026-09-08): 10-30 s windows with at least one end-of-turn event in which one participant never takes a turn — two takers (A/B, lowest PC is A) and a listener (L). The events are `{TPC_EOT}` = `F:\Data\GazePattern\EoT_2022`, built by `{GAZETURN}` = `F:\Code\scnpro\prototype\prototype\gazeturn` (`gazeturn/labeling/eot_events_2022.py`) from `Caption/*_sentence.csv`; the doc's "End-of-turn events" section has the schema and provenance. Speaker codes are true PC numbers. Event times, caption times, the trimmed wavs under `{TPC_DATA}/audio_text` and the converted npz all share one clock from the same instant, verified on all 23 sessions. The captions are automatic and carry `[Music]` rows and microphone bleed; a tag-only row rejects a window, bleed can only be caught by reading `--inspect`. Motion glitches are reported per window, not filtered (user's call): the legs are dealt with later. **Which participant becomes what is undecided** — the user wants to watch full original clips with all three first. Nothing is exported yet.

**Sessions are watched in their own scene** (2026-09-08): `GazeControl → 3People → Set Up Replay Scene` builds `Assets/Scenes/ThreePartyReplay.unity`, and `ThreePartyReplay` plays all three participants of one session at once off a single clock — the same one-DSP-instant scheduling `RecordedConversation` uses, because three microphone tracks of one room drifting a frame apart is audible on every overlap. Not `TriadScene`: the study's starting viewpoint is a controlled variable, and this camera orbits, since the three face each other and a fixed camera always has someone's back to it. **The room places the bodies, not the scene** — the motion players run with `RootTranslationRelative` off, so the three seats' transforms must stay identity or the participant leaves the room they were recorded in. Audio is read straight off disk as just the requested window (`WavSegment`, pure and unit-tested; a whole ten-minute stereo session decodes to 230 MB per participant, so Unity's own loaders are not usable). `ThreePartyOverlay` names each body, shows its live caption and marks the annotated events. No gaze policy runs, the eyes stay where the mocap left them, and nothing is logged: this is for deciding who becomes what, not for measuring.

**`GazeControl → 3People → Session Browser`** picks the date, session and window, lists the finder's ranking from `output/3people_segments.csv` as a starting point, and **reads a window out as a transcript with its events marked before anything plays** — the editor-side counterpart of `--inspect`, and the only way to catch the microphone bleed that puts a phantom line, and so a phantom event, on the wrong track. Play whole session ignores the ranking, which is what watching the originals means.

**The participant replaces the listener** (user, 2026-09-08). Of the three recorded people, the one who never takes a turn — no end-of-turn event names them; at most a backchannel — becomes the study participant's seat, and the other two become the agents. `ThreePartyReplay` derives that participant from the events inside the window (`ListenerPc` overrides it), and `ThreePartyViewpoint` (pure, unit-tested) measures the seat from the recording rather than authoring it: **position is the mean eye position over the whole window**, not the pose at any one frame, and **the opening facing is the direction to the midpoint of the two speakers' eyes at the first frame**, so the clip opens with both of them in view whatever the room layout was.

**It is the converted body's head, not the recorded person's, and everybody keeps the default body** (user, 2026-09-08). The converter poses a **default SMPL-X male with betas all zero** (all 69 clips, the women included) at the participant's real room position, so horizontally the seat is theirs and vertically it is the template's — nothing in the motion layer reads `betas` at all. `ground_offset_m` in each npz is exactly that discrepancy at the pelvis, and it runs from **−1 cm to +17 cm** across these participants: Clayton and Benjamin are the default body's height, Karthik 3 cm shorter, Crystal 10 cm, Joseph 9 cm, May 17 cm. Crystal's own eyes were around **1.54 m**; the seat measures **1.71 m**.

**Recovering the real heights is not needed, because the gaze is derived by target and not by absolute angle** (the user's reason, and it is the one that settles it). Every gaze decision in the system names a target: the prototypes are role-coded, `GazeController` aims at the target's `LookAtAnchor` in whatever geometry is rendered, and the participant's own gaze resolves through a 0.15 m head sphere. The flattened heights (≈7-9° of elevation at a metre) change the rendered angle, not which target anything names, and stay well inside `GazeController`'s ±25° pitch clamp. It is also identical for every participant, so it is a constant of the apparatus rather than a confound — and seating the participant at their real height under default-bodied speakers would have *opened* a gap the recording did not have. Per-participant shape (the VSK anthropometrics, or the deferred marker fit) remains available and would correct the seat for free, since `ThreePartyViewpoint` measures the posed skeleton rather than storing a constant. Yaw only, as in `ViewRecentring` — pitch and roll come from the headset against gravity and are already right. Eyes rather than the head bone, the same two joints `ParticipantEyeHeight` measures. **V** in the replay stands in that seat with the listener's body hidden; dragging turns the head and moves it nowhere (the study rig itself tracks translation too since 2026-09-08).

**Seven clips are chosen** (user, 2026-09-08) — see `Assets/Docs/3people-motion-sources.md`; **the session runs five of them**, `study2_c5` and `study2_c6` dropped the same day (user), so a session is 15 takes and Fig7e and Fig8e are never played. All are 10.9-18.6 s with exactly one annotated event, six a turn-taking and one an interruption, and in five of the seven the listener says nothing at all.

**The study is being redesigned as the triadic prototypes played in a triad, with the participant's gaze as an outcome measure** (user, 2026-09-08). `Assets/Docs/study2-design.md` is the design and the handoff; read it before touching this work. In one line: the participant stands in the listener's seat, the agents play the prototypes' speaker columns **baked and open-loop exactly as in study 1**, and the participant's tracked gaze is measured against the listener column rather than used to choose anything. A gaze-driven *retrieval* of the prototype was settled on first and withdrawn the same day: only four of the fifteen prototypes ever aim an agent at the listener (60 of 900 agent-frames), so the selection would have been invisible to the participant while costing `GazeTrackBaker`'s reproducibility — §2 of the doc has the table. Contingent completion (branch on returned gaze) is the deferred study 3. This supersedes the three-condition, fifteen-clip design described in `study-session-notes.md` ("Running a session").

**Study 2 replaces study 1's corpus and nothing else** (user, 2026-09-08), and that is how it is built:

- **`Tools/export_3people_segments.py`** writes the seven chosen windows as ordinary segments, `Assets/DemoSegments/study2_c1`…`c7` (schema `gazecontrol.demo-segment/3`), so the runner, the baker, the clip browser and the session play them through study 1's code. **Speaker codes are remapped onto the study-1 contract**: the two takers are speaker 1 (agent A, the lower PC) and 2, the listener is speaker 0, the user; the true PC and subject name stay on each agent record. The listener's audio is not exported (nobody plays it). Events are scheduled by the finder's own lead/tail rule, so `study2_c4`, which ends on a second event, schedules one, like the rest. Voices are measured as in study 1, and a measurement in the unclassifiable 155-175 Hz gap is settled from the corpus's register (`VOICE_BY_SUBJECT`: Maryum and May), which TalkingWithHands could not do.
- **The seat is in the JSON, and it is the apex of an equilateral triangle on the two takers** (user, 2026-09-08 evening; it was the listener's own mean eye position that morning). `seat` is the point on the listener's side of the takers' base at the base's own length from both, facing their midpoint, computed by forward kinematics of the converter's default body over the whole window and written in Unity's frame (x mirrored); the takers keep their recorded distance and bearing to each other, so the triangle's side is the recording's (1.14-1.77 m across the seven clips). The recorded listeners sat off that apex by 0.2-1.1 m and at unequal distances from the two takers, which is why the seat moved: one agent read nearer and larger than the other, differently per clip. The listener's real seat stays in the record as `seat.listenerSeat`. It is done once at export, so the study scene needs no third body.
- **The scene moves the room, never the participant.** `Assets/Scenes/Study2Scene.unity` is a copy of TriadScene made by `GazeControl → Study 2 → Set Up Scene` (`Study2SceneSetup`, re-runnable): AgentA and AgentB sit under one `Room` object with identity local transforms and raw root translation, and `RecordedRoomPlacement` on that object turns and slides the room per clip so the seat lands on the User vertex and the seat's facing on the vertex's forward axis (`RoomPlacement`, pure and unit-tested; yaw only, floor stays floor). The participant's viewpoint is therefore the identical point, height and facing as study 1; what differs between clips is the room's geometry, which is the recording's. `RecordedConversation.Placement` applies it as the segment loads, and refuses a seat segment in a vertex scene or a vertex segment in a room scene. `PlayAsync` now poses the bodies at the first frame during the audio lead, because under a raw-mode room an armed player otherwise holds the bind pose at the room origin.
- **Each agent's head is levelled** (user, 2026-09-08): the exporter measures the mean elevation of the head joint's forward axis over the window per person (`headPitchDegrees`; c1 has Maryum 12° up and Justice 9° down, the tall and the short looking at each other), and `SmplxMotionPlayer.HeadPitchCorrectionDegrees`, set by `RecordedConversation` at load, post-multiplies that pitch onto the head's local rotation each frame about the head's own lateral axis. On the default body the recorded tilt points at nobody; nods and turns are kept, only the mean goes. Verified live to 0.03°. Zero for segments without the field, so study 1's clips are untouched.
- **Class-matched pool** (user, 2026-09-08): `ProposedGazePolicy` draws from the event's own class, as in study 1.
- **Baking the whole seed matrix**: `GazeControl → Study 2 → Bake Seeds 1-12` queues every condition at seeds 1-12 for every conversation the session runner lists (252 play sessions for seven clips, about two hours), so a seed per clip can be *chosen from what exists* on the morning of a study. `GazeTrackBaker`'s queue now carries segment, seed and condition per take, **validates each track as its play session ends** (exists, right segment and seed, at least duration × 60 decisions per agent) and re-queues a short one up to three attempts, then writes `output/bake_report_<stamp>.txt`. It also **disables `StudySessionRunner` for the duration** and restores it: that component's `Awake` clears the conversation's autoplay on every plain Play, which is right for a participant run and left a bake waiting on a framing screen; `Awake` now returns early when the component is disabled (after the launch request, which is what enables it for a real session).
- **The questionnaire's counts follow the session** (2026-09-08): the framing body and `nextGroupFormat` take `{0}` groups and `{1}` versions, filled from `QuestionnaireSession.GroupCount` and `StudySessionRunner.ConditionCount`. Item wording is unchanged and still study 1's.
- **Not done**: no seed has been chosen per clip; the per-clip items were written for study 1; the seven clips have not been watched from the seat in a headset.
