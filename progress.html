In progress User study design
design settled 2026-08-21, written up in Assets/Docs/user-study-design.md (tracked, beside baseline-spec.md, because it is what the study gets built from). Scene 1 only; scene 2 is discarded from the study for now
  • Within-subjects, 5 conversations × 3 methods = 15 clips per participant, blocked by conversation: the three methods of one conversation play back-to-back, then the participant ranks them. Method order counterbalanced per participant from a pre-generated schedule — block *b* of ordinal *i* takes permutation (i + b) mod 6 — while conversation order stays fixed, since it cannot enter the within-block contrast and a fixed order keeps every trial identifiable. N = 18 analysed (P05-P22), a multiple of 6 so the marginals close exactly; P01-P04 are pilots _(2026-08-31, §0.1)_.
  • Live VR, not video playback — TriadScene ported to XR, the participant standing at the third triad vertex. That is what gives the "seemed aware I was there" item its force: an agent's gaze lands on the participant's own eyes rather than on a camera.
  • Primary DV is the per-block ranking ("rank the three by how natural the characters' *eye movements* were, 1-3, no ties") — immune to scale-use bias and far more sensitive than absolute ratings for a gaze-only difference. Plus four 7-point items per video (gaze naturalness / boundary gaze timing / mutual engagement / being noticed) and a free-text discomfort probe per block.
  • Every item names eye movements, never "behaviour" (user's correction 2026-08-21): body motion is identical across methods, so an item admitting motion is partly rating a constant, which dilutes the effect. Two drafted items were cut for this and for scope — an overall-believability item (admits motion) and a next-speaker-prediction item (contradicts the paper's own position that gaze alone cannot predict turns, and the participant never takes a turn).
  • ~~Eye tracking finally populates mutual_gaze_with_human — four measures: mutual gaze proportion, gaze allocation heatmap, gaze distribution, gaze-return latency.~~ Dropped 2026-08-31: under an instruction to watch the characters' eyes, with no turn ever available, the participant's gaze is task-driven scanning rather than third-party gaze, and gaze-return latency is floored by it. Replaced by distributional fidelity of the generated gaze against gaze_events_md6.csv, computed over the baked tracks. The capture stays built; no analysis is promised on it.
  • Analysis aggregates to participant level before testing (Friedman + Kendall's *W* on mean ranks; one LMM per Likert construct with participant *and* conversation as random effects). Explicitly not per-clip or per-gaze-sample tests — that error is what produces impressive F values beside effect sizes near 0.0001 in the comparable literature.
  • ~~Open: pilot before recruiting; formal power calculation.~~ Both done 2026-08-31 — P01-P04 served as the pilot, and the calculation fixed N = 18 with its sensitivity registered.
