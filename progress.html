  • Re-targeting cadence: the corpus README claims 0.6-0.94 s "bracketing Shintani's 0.7 s", but aversion_segments.csv gives per-segment median 0.22 s, mean 0.36 s (2-3 segments per event, ~0.75 s mean aversion). Sample segment durations from the empirical distribution rather than any constant, which sidesteps the conflict.
  • Use lognormal durations, not χ². The corpus authors tested both: across role × target × min_dwell, 0 of 45 cells give χ² an interior mode (fitted dn 0.88–1.78), and lognormal fits better in 8 of 9 cells (mean KS 0.051 vs 0.070). Shintani's χ² degenerates toward exponential on this data. This lands back on baseline-spec.md §2's original log-normal choice. Also: dloc is an artifact of the min_dwell filter (0.1 s), not a property of gaze.
  • The "twitchy dwell" worry is resolved. Real durations are roughly double the geometric ones implied by the transition matrix — mean 0.65–1.11 s, median 0.40–0.58 s, p90 1.3–2.5 s (vs 0.30–0.53 s geometric), with a genuine long tail. Gaze at the *speaker* is longest (~1.1 s), matching Shintani and Mutlu's ~0.98 s.
  • ad is NOT an annotated addressee — it is second_speaker, a hindsight label for whoever actually spoke next, which participants did not have at the time. first_speaker[k] == second_speaker[k-1] holds only 47.9% of the time, so these do not form a floor-passing chain. sp is sound; ad is loose and sd inherits that looseness. Shintani's Addressee is a different variable — say so when reporting.
  • Aversion direction was recovered from the raw head-mounted eye tracker (34/38 sessions) rather than annotated: 9-way marginals per role plus the 9×9 first→second conditional. Measured re-targeting period 0.6–0.94 s brackets Shintani's 0.7 s constant. Note our distribution is flatter than theirs and lateral-down dominates (ld/rd ≈ 0.15) rather than pure down; u is rarest (0.05).
